# Instagram steps (all TODO verify against Meta's Instagram Messaging docs)
**Prerequisites:** Instagram account switched to **Business/Professional**, a Facebook Page, the Meta app from the WhatsApp guide.
1. **Switch Instagram to a Professional (Business) account** in the Instagram app settings.
2. **Link it to your Facebook Page** (Page settings → Linked accounts → Instagram). TODO verify the menu path.
3. **Allow message access:** in Instagram settings → Messages → Connected tools, allow access to messages. TODO verify.
4. **Add the Instagram (Messaging) product** to your Meta app and connect the Page/IG account.
5. **Set the webhook:** `https://<your-sell-safe-host>/webhooks/instagram`, same verify token. Subscribe to `messages`.
6. **Generate a Page access token** and add it to Sell-Safe (never paste it in chats).
7. **DM your shop from another account** → **Check connection**.
Note: going public needs Meta app review and permissions (e.g. instagram_manage_messages). TODO verify the current permission names.
