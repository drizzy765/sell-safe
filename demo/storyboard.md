# Sell-Safe Demo Storyboard

Target runtime: 2:45. Maximum 3 minutes. No real phone numbers or account numbers.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:20)

- Visual: Presenter on camera, then a fake transfer screenshot pops in beside them.
- Voiceover: "Last year someone almost walked away with my goods using a fake transfer screenshot. It happens to sellers every day."

## Scene 2 — Intro (0:20–0:35)

- Visual: Logo + one-line pitch card.
- Voiceover: "Sell-Safe is a WhatsApp shop assistant powered by Meta Muse that sells for you and catches fake payments."

## Scene 3 — Catalog (0:35–1:00)

- Visual: Seller sends a bag photo in WhatsApp; a product card with price appears.
- Voiceover: "Snap a photo, Muse vision writes the listing."

## Scene 4 — Buyer chat (1:00–1:30)

- Visual: Buyer messages in Pidgin: "Abeg how much for the black bag?" Bot replies with price. Then buyer says "I go take 1" and order ref appears.
- Voiceover: "Buyers ask in Pidgin or Yoruba; it answers from your real catalog and takes the order."

## Scene 5 — Fake caught (1:30–2:05)

- Visual: Edited screenshot sent; bot replies SUSPICIOUS with reasons and DON'T RELEASE.
- Voiceover: "The receipt looks real. Sell-Safe checks the bank side: no money arrived, the reference was reused."

## Scene 6 — Real pay (2:05–2:25)

- Visual: Paystack test transfer arrives; bot replies CONFIRMED by bank webhook.
- Voiceover: "Real money? Confirmed in seconds, straight from the bank webhook."

## Scene 7 — Scale (2:25–2:40)

- Visual: Instagram DM with same flow, then daily summary card showing naira saved from fakes.
- Voiceover: "Same brain on Instagram and Messenger."

## Scene 8 — Close (2:40–2:50)

- Visual: Architecture slide + Meta logos, plus benchmark headline: "Zero false CONFIRMED on synthetic fakes."
- Voiceover: "Built on Muse and Meta's platforms. Sell-Safe: sell more, get scammed less."
